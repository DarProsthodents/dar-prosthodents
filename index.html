Dar Prosthodents is a leading dental laboratory network operating in Lahore and Karachi, Pakistan. Founded by Mr. Idrees Ahmed Dar in 1970, the company offers a comprehensive range of dental laboratory services, including restorative, prosthetic, implant, and orthodontic work, alongside advanced CAD/CAM solutions.

Computer-Aided Design (CAD) and Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM)

Experienced technicians and advanced computer technology working together. CAD/CAM zirconia gives your patients a stronger, more natural and more tissue-friendly restoration than PFM.

Strength Meets Aesthetics
Zirconia offers superior strength with a natural appearance, so a restoration can be tough and lifelike at once.
Tooth-Conserving Design
High strength allows thinner restorations. That means less tooth reduction and more natural tooth structure kept.
Gum-Friendly Contours
Zirconia is biocompatible and tissue-friendly, supporting better gum health and lowering the risk of allergic reactions.
Precise Marginal Fit
Digital design and milling give a consistent, accurate fit for crowns, bridges and implant restorations.
